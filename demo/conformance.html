Median of five. The GPU column includes gl.finish(), so it is wall time and not a queued command buffer; the readback column is what a conformance comparison or an export costs on top.

Tolerance 1 is a floor, not a licence. GLSL ES 3.00 has no precise qualifier, so a driver may reassociate float expressions and bit-exactness can only be measured, never promised. Read real % beside every verdict: a shader that is uniformly one greylevel wrong shows a high any % with max Δ 1, which is rounding, while a real bug shows a non-zero real %. Do not raise the tolerance to turn a row green.
